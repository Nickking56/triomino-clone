    Official rules (Deluxe Tri-Ominos)
    First to 400 points wins the match, played over several rounds. Each side is dealt 9 tiles; the rest form the pile.

    Opening: whoever holds the highest triple (5-5-5 highest, down to 1-1-1) opens with it for the tile's value +10 — or, if that same player also holds 0-0-0, they open with that instead for +40 (0 + a 30pt triple-zero bonus + the normal 10pt bonus). With no triple in either hand, the single highest-value tile opens instead, for no bonus.

    Each turn, join one tile onto a matching side already on the table — if you have a match, you must play it. A tile scores the sum of its three numbers, plus:
    • Bridge (+40) — matches 2 sides at once, or matches 1 side whose opposite point already lines up with a value fixed elsewhere on the table.
    • Hexagon (+50) — closes a ring of 6 tiles around a point.

    No match? Buy from the pile, −5 points each, as many times as needed until you can play. Still stuck once the pile runs dry → −10 and your turn ends.

    Round ends when a hand empties (that player scores +25 plus the pips left in the other hand, loser keeps their score) or both sides are blocked with the pile empty (whoever holds the fewest tiles adds the other's pip total and subtracts their own, no bonus). Scores carry across rounds until someone reaches 400.
